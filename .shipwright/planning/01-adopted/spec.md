## Abstract

Wartgeld Generator Zug is a small single-page web app that lets midwives in the Canton of Zug (Switzerland) create the invoice for the cantonal midwife standby allowance ("Hebammenwartgeld"). The midwife enters the mother's name, home address and date of birth, ticks which of the two reimbursable services she provided (care during a home birth, care of the mother at home after birth; CHF 400 each), and the app produces a ready-to-send PDF invoice addressed to the municipal administration responsible for the mother's place of residence. The invoice can be previewed, printed or saved.

The midwife's own details (sender block with contact and IBAN, place of issue, an optional fixed invoice date, and a hand-drawn signature) are entered once on a settings page and reused for every invoice. These settings and all patient data stay in the user's browser; nothing about the patient is stored on a server. The only data that leaves the browser is the street text typed into the address field, which is sent to the Swiss Post address autocomplete service (via the project's own lookup service). The responsible municipality is proposed in the browser from the official Swiss locality list shipped with the app, and the midwife can change it.

The UI is German-only. The app is deployed as a static site on Netlify; its backend is one Supabase edge function that proxies the Swiss Post autocomplete API with origin restriction and rate limiting.

## Acceptance Criteria

### FR-01.01 — Invoice entry form

_Source: enrichment._

- Given the invoice form is open, when the midwife submits with a first or last name shorter than 2 characters, no address, an address without a selected postcode, or no date of birth, then no invoice is produced and each problem is shown in German next to the form.
- (E) Given an address in 6319 Allenwinden, when it is picked, then `Zuständige Gemeinde` shows Baar without a hint (iterate-2026-10-21-zug-municipality-data).
- (E) Given an address in 6300 Zug, when it is picked, then `Zuständige Gemeinde` shows Zug without a hint.
- (E) Given an address in 6314 Neuägeri, when it is picked, then no municipality is pre-selected, its four municipalities are listed first and a hint asks the midwife to choose; submitting before choosing shows `Bitte wählen Sie die zuständige Gemeinde` and produces no invoice.
- (E) Given an address in 6340 Sihlbrugg, when it is picked, then nothing is pre-selected and a hint says the locality lies partly outside the Canton of Zug (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given an address in 8836 Bennau, 6417 Sattel, 6418 Rothenthurm or 5642 Mühlau, when the midwife submits, then the form shows `Diese PLZ liegt nicht im Kanton Zug`, proposes no municipality and produces no invoice (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given a municipality was chosen by hand, when only the house number changes, then the choice is kept; when an address in another locality is picked, the new proposal replaces it; when the address is typed over instead of picked, postcode, town and municipality are cleared.
- (E) Given a postcode without any Zug locality (e.g. 8001 Zürich), when the midwife submits, then the form shows `Diese PLZ liegt nicht im Kanton Zug` and produces no invoice.
- (E) Given the services are ticked, when the midwife looks at the form, then a total shows CHF 0.00, CHF 400.00 or CHF 800.00, the same amount the PDF prints (iterate-2026-10-22-redesign-invoice-page).
- (E) Given a municipality is chosen, when the midwife looks below `Zuständige Gemeinde`, then `Rechnung geht an` names the recipient, with the municipality name added to a generic title (e.g. `Gemeindeverwaltung Menzingen`) (iterate-2026-10-22-redesign-invoice-page).
- (E) Given the midwife submits invalid entries, when the form refuses, then a summary above the form counts the problems, each entry moves focus to its field without leaving the page, and each field shows its message below it; while she is still typing, no summary is shown (iterate-2026-10-22-redesign-invoice-page).

### FR-01.02 — Address suggestions for the Canton of Zug

_Source: enrichment._

- Given the midwife has typed at least 2 characters of a street name, when suggestions arrive, then only addresses with a Zug postcode are listed and selecting one fills the address, postcode and town fields.
- (E) Given the midwife types `Bahnhofstr 1`, when suggestions arrive, then `Bahnhofstrasse 1` in Zug, Baar and Steinhausen come first, at most 10 suggestions are shown, and none is in Bennau, Sattel, Rothenthurm, Mühlau or outside the Zug locality list (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given the lookup service fails or Swiss Post does not answer in time, when the midwife types, then `Fehler bei der Adresssuche` is shown and no suggestion appears (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given the midwife types several letters quickly, when she pauses, then one lookup is sent for the full text, and suggestions for earlier text are not offered meanwhile (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-static-hosting).
- (E) Given the suggestion list is open (with results, while loading or with no result), when the midwife presses Escape, then the list closes; the list credits `Adressdaten: Schweizerische Post` (iterate-2026-10-22-redesign-invoice-page).

### FR-01.10 — Protected address lookup service

_Source: enrichment._

- (E) Given a request from an origin not on the allow-list, when it reaches the service, then it is refused (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given the per-instance cap of lookups in one minute is used up, when another lookup arrives, then it is refused until the minute is over (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given a request with a missing origin, a look-alike of a deploy-preview origin (other site, extra label, http, other port), another method, a body over 1 KB or invalid fields, when it reaches the service, then it is refused with 403, 405, 413 or 400 and Swiss Post is not called (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given any lookup, when the service logs it, then the log holds only counts, status and duration, never the typed text or Swiss Post's answer, and the response is marked not cacheable (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given the built site, when its files are checked, then they contain no Swiss Post credential, no `POST_APIM` name and no Supabase address (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-post-proxy).
- (E) Given one visitor sends more than 50 lookups within 10 seconds, when the next lookup arrives, then Bunny answers 429 for 60 seconds while pages still load; calls straight to the service's own address are limited the same way (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-static-hosting).
- (E) Given the site at wartgeld.nidea.ch, when it sends a lookup, then the service accepts that origin, and the old Netlify site and its previews keep working (iterate-2026-10-21-bunny-static-hosting).
