## Abstract

Wartgeld Generator Zug is a small single-page web app that lets midwives in the Canton of Zug (Switzerland) create the invoice for the cantonal midwife standby allowance ("Hebammenwartgeld"). The midwife enters the mother's name, home address and date of birth, ticks which of the two reimbursable services she provided (care during a home birth, care of the mother at home after birth; CHF 400 each), and the app produces a ready-to-send PDF invoice addressed to the municipal administration responsible for the mother's place of residence. The invoice can be previewed, printed or saved.

The midwife's own details (sender block with contact and IBAN, place of issue, an optional fixed invoice date, and a hand-drawn signature) are entered once on a settings page and reused for every invoice. These settings and all patient data stay in the user's browser; nothing about the patient is stored on a server. The only data that leaves the browser is the street text typed into the address field, which is sent to the Swiss Post address autocomplete service (via the project's own lookup service). The responsible municipality is proposed in the browser from the official Swiss locality list shipped with the app, and the midwife can change it.

The UI is German-only. The app is deployed as a static site on Netlify; its backend is one Supabase edge function that proxies the Swiss Post autocomplete API with origin restriction and rate limiting.

## Acceptance Criteria

### FR-01.01 — Invoice entry form

_Source: enrichment._

- Given the invoice form is open, when the midwife submits with a first or last name shorter than 2 characters, no address, an address without a selected postcode, or no date of birth, then no invoice is produced and each problem is shown in German next to the form.
- (E) Given an address in 6319 Allenwinden is picked, then `Zuständige Gemeinde` shows Baar; given 6300 Zug, it shows Zug without a hint (iterate-2026-10-21-zug-municipality-data).
- (E) Given an address in 6314 Neuägeri is picked, then no municipality is pre-selected, its four municipalities are listed first and a hint asks the midwife to choose; submitting before choosing shows `Bitte wählen Sie die zuständige Gemeinde` and produces no invoice.
- (E) Given an address in 8836 Bennau is picked, then nothing is pre-selected and a hint says the locality lies partly outside the Canton of Zug.
- (E) Given a municipality was chosen by hand, when only the house number changes, then the choice is kept; when an address in another locality is picked, the new proposal replaces it; when the address is typed over instead of picked, postcode, town and municipality are cleared.
- (E) Given a postcode without any Zug locality (e.g. 8001 Zürich), then the form shows `Diese PLZ liegt nicht im Kanton Zug` and produces no invoice.

### FR-01.02 — Address suggestions for the Canton of Zug

_Source: enrichment._

- Given the midwife has typed at least 2 characters of a street name, when suggestions arrive, then only addresses with a Zug postcode are listed and selecting one fills the address, postcode and town fields.

### FR-01.03 — Invoice PDF generation

_Source: enrichment._

- Given a valid form with a Zug postcode and both services ticked, when the midwife clicks 'Rechnung Generieren', then exactly one PDF is produced, shown in the preview, addressed to that municipality's administration, and totalling CHF 800.
- (E) Given the midwife chose Menzingen for a Neuägeri address, when she generates the invoice, then it is addressed to Gemeindeverwaltung, Soziales und Gesundheit, Postfach, 6313 Menzingen, and no request goes to Supabase or any other server (iterate-2026-10-21-zug-municipality-data).
- (E) For each of the 11 Zug municipalities, the recipient lines on the invoice equal the administration addresses used before the change (Supabase export of 2026-10-21).

### FR-01.04 — Print and save the invoice

_Source: enrichment._

- Given an invoice has been generated for 'Anna Muster', when the midwife clicks 'Speichern', then a file named 'Anna_Muster.pdf' is downloaded; when she clicks 'Drucken', the browser print dialog opens for that invoice.

### FR-01.05 — Reset the form

_Source: enrichment._

- Given the form is filled and an invoice was generated, when the midwife clicks 'Formular Zurücksetzen', then all fields, the address search and the preview are cleared and a confirmation is shown.

### FR-01.06 — Invoice issuer details

_Source: enrichment._

- Given the midwife saved sender details and a fixed invoice date on the settings page, when she generates an invoice, then the PDF shows those sender details and that date; when the date is cleared, the PDF shows today's date.

### FR-01.07 — Handwritten signature

_Source: enrichment._

- Given a signature was saved, when the midwife changes and saves her sender details, then the signature is still present on the next generated invoice; when she clears the signature, the next invoice has none.

### FR-01.08 — About, privacy and contact information

_Source: enrichment._

- Given the user opens the information page, then the purpose, usage steps, privacy statement, disclaimer and contact address are shown in German, with a way back to the form.

### FR-01.09 — Light and dark appearance

_Source: enrichment._

- Given the user selects dark mode, when the page is reloaded, then the app is still shown in dark mode.

### FR-01.10 — Protected address lookup service

_Source: enrichment._

- Given a request from an origin not on the allow-list, then it is refused; given more than the per-client limit of lookups in one minute, then further lookups are refused until the window passes.
